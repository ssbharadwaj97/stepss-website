What We Do

Everything Under One Roof

From your first semester to your last round of interviews, we've got most of it covered.

Thesis, Dissertation and Final Project Guidance

Your thesis is probably the hardest thing you'll do in your Masters. We've helped students get through this at 30+ universities in the US, UK and Australia. We guide you through every part of it, from choosing a strong topic to getting the writing right.

  • Research question scoping and literature review strategy
  • Research design, methodology and data analysis
  • Chapter-by-chapter feedback and academic writing support
  • Dissertation defence preparation and mock Q&A sessions
  • Capstone project planning, implementation and presentation
Doubt Resolution

Sometimes you're stuck on a concept and just need someone to explain it properly. Book a session, tell us what's confusing you, and we'll work through it together until it clicks.

  • On-demand subject-specific sessions
  • Worked examples and concept walkthroughs
  • Exam technique and revision planning
Skill-Building Courses

We teach practical courses that actually matter. Things like our Palantir Foundry course, Python, ML, SQL, cloud and more. These are taught by people who use these tools at work, not just from a textbook.

  • Palantir Foundry (industry-specific, very niche)
  • Data Science, ML and AI fundamentals to advanced
  • Python, SQL, Cloud and Web Development
  • Business Analytics, Finance and MBA courses
Resume Writing

A generic resume doesn't work well in the US and UK job market. We write your resume from scratch, tailored for the role and country you're applying in, not just a filled-in template.

  • ATS keyword optimisation for each role
  • Impact-focused bullet points with real numbers
  • Multiple versions for different types of roles
LinkedIn Profile Makeover

Most students have a pretty weak LinkedIn. We rewrite it so recruiters actually find you and want to reach out, not the other way around.

  • Keyword-rich headline and about section
  • Experience reframing for international markets
  • Outreach approach and connection strategy
Portfolio Building

If you're going into tech or data, a good portfolio can make a big difference. We help you figure out what projects to show, set it all up, and explain things in a way that actually impresses hiring managers.

  • GitHub portfolio curation and project readmes
  • Personal website setup and project write-ups
  • Case study structuring for business and MBA roles
